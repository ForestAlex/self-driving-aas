# 1 - In-vitro kit considerations and preparations

We use the ΔtRNA, aa NEB _in-vitro_ translation kit (# E6840S, https://www.neb.com/de-de/products/e6840-purexpress-aa-trna-kit) and have **2 kits** available. 

**Composition of the kit**

- 25 µl Solution A       (#B6841A) 
- **75 µl Solution B**   (#P0760)   ➜ limiting factor, so we have a total of **150 µl** available
- 25 µl tRNAs            (#N6842A)
- 25 µl amino acid mix   (#N6843A)       


Typically, I use 12.5 µl but we can scale down to 10 µl. This is what is necessary for one reaction:

**Required for one minimal reaction (10 µl)**

- [X] 1 µl aa-mix (at 100 mM) 
- [X] 1 µl tRNA
- [X] 2 µl Sol. A
- [X] 3 µl Sol. B
- [X] 1 µl MilliQ water
- [X] 1 µl mNeonGreen DNA (145.3 ng/µl)

----------------
total: 10 µl

This should be put into a black 384 well plate (for later fluorescence). At maximum, with two kits, we can run **150/3 = 50** reactions. We have **81 amino acids** to test. We need **1 positive control** and **5 negative controls** = **87 reactions per run**, limited by the reagent kit we run **40 = 6 contr. + 34 amino acids** in the first round and **4 to optimize + 6 as random choice** in the second round.

For the amino acids, we add 1 µl of a 100 mM mixture of amino acids (= 5 mM per each of the 20, absent is often the one we like to substitute so then it is only 19 components). For the amino acid to test, we add a 5x higher amount and also 1 µl of a 25 mM solution.

----------------
total: 8 µl
