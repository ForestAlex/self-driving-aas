# 5 Post-test-day analysis

After the fluorescence readouts generated as data during the self-driving hackathon, the 10 µl volumes of proteins should be saved at -20°C. For the further analysis, it is important to understand that new amino acids might shift the fluorescence properties drastically (and thereby there might be protein, but the fluorescence measured is a wrong proxy readout).

Two analysis will follow:

- [X] **1a.** Spectral scanning of EXC (absorption) between 300-700 nm with the full 10 µl in Tübingen.
- [X] **1b.** Spectral scanning of EM (emission) between 450-750 nm with the full 10 µl in Tübingen.
- [X] **2.** Denaturing 15-25% MES-SDS gel to see the gel bands (= pure protein readout).

In the end, the hackathon results should then be:

- [X] Comparison of which amino acids can get included (and do not shift fluorescence properties).
- [X] Comparison of which amino acids can get included but shift fluorescence properties.
- [X] Comparison of which amino acids can get included, but show no fluorescence and only appear on the gel (= unfolded).
- [X] Comparison between different only slightly differing samples (e.g. +Tyr from our lab vs. +Tyr delivered) to verify the stability of the method.
