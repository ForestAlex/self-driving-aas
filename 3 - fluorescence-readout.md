# Fluorescence readout

We read out the fluorescence from the **384-well plate** with 440 nm (EXC) and 500 nm (EMISSION). During running we pause every hour to test. After the run, a spectral scan should be recorded. This was tested and successful, the plates used were _Thermo Fisher: (384 well polypropylene plate black w/o kud, non sterile)_ (#264576).

**Protocol** (tested on Synergy plate readers)
- [ ] 384 well-plate to insert
- [ ] SHAKE medium 0:30 (MM:SS)
- [ ] SELECT wells with liquid
- [ ] FIXED EXCITATION: 440/9.0 nm
- [ ] FIXED EMISSION: 500/9.0 nm
- [ ] OPTICS: Top
- [ ] GAIN: 100
- [ ] LIGHT SOURCE: Xenon Flash (Monochromators)
- [ ] READ SPEED: normal, DELAY: 100 msec
- [ ] READ HEIGHT: 8 mm 

**Protocol for spectral scan** (later, after 4h, tested on Synergy plate readers)
- [ ] 384 well-plate to insert
- [ ] SHAKE medium 0:30 (MM:SS)
- [ ] SELECT wells with liquid
- [ ] 1a. FIXED EXCITATION: 440/9.0 nm
- [ ] 1b. EMISSION START: 450/9.0 nm, STOP: 700 nm, STEP: 10 nm
- [ ] 2a. FIXED EXCITATION: 495/9.0 nm
- [ ] 2b. EMISSION START: 500/9.0 nm, STOP: 700 nm, STEP: 10 nm
- [ ] 2a. FIXED EXCITATION: 560/9.0 nm
- [ ] 2b. EMISSION START: 580/9.0 nm, STOP: 700 nm, STEP: 10 nm
- [ ] OPTICS: Top
- [ ] GAIN: 100
- [ ] LIGHT SOURCE: Xenon Flash (Monochromators)
- [ ] READ SPEED: normal, DELAY: 100 msec
- [ ] READ HEIGHT: 8 mm
